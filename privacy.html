Gizlilik Politikası

Blackhole Storm · Lias Labs · Son güncelleme: 4 Ekim 2026

Blackhole Storm'da hesap ve analiz (analytics) aracı yoktur. Web sürümünde reklam yoktur; Android uygulamasında reklamlar Google AdMob ile gösterilir (aşağıda). Verini satmayız. Oyunun verileri telefonunda kalır; sunucumuza yalnızca aşağıda anlatılan dünya sıralaması (Ayarlar'dan kapatılabilir) ve Google Play satın alma onayı için sınırlı bilgi gider.

Cihazında saklananlar

  • Oyuncu adı ve avatar (kendin seçersin, gerçek adın olması gerekmez)
  • Skorlar, rekorlar, seviye ilerlemesi, yıldız ve elmas bakiyesi, başarımlar, günlük görevler
  • Ayarlar (müzik, efekt sesleri, titreşim, kayıt tercihi)

Bunlar yalnızca telefonunun tarayıcı depolamasında (localStorage) durur; dünya sıralaması için gönderilenler (aşağıda) dışında hiçbir sunucuya gönderilmez. Uygulamayı silmek veya site verilerini temizlemek hepsini kalıcı olarak siler.

Dünya sıralaması (Günün Fırtınası)

Hayatta Kal modunda bir koşu bittiğinde, Ayarlar › Dünya sıralamasına katıl açıksa oyun şu bilgileri sıralama sunucumuza (Cloudflare) gönderir: cihazında rastgele üretilmiş bir kurulum numarası (seninle ilişkilendirilmez), oyunda yazdığın oyuncu adı, skorun, koşunun süresi ve tarih. Bunlar yalnızca günlük sıralamayı göstermek ve ilk 3'e ödül vermek için kullanılır; en fazla 90 gün saklanır, sonra silinir. E-posta, konum, cihaz kimliği veya reklam kimliği gönderilmez. Katılmak istemezsen ayarı kapat; kaydının silinmesini istersen bize yaz.

Uygunsuz oyuncu adları sunucuda otomatik süzülür ve "PİLOT" olarak gösterilir. Sıralamadaki bir ismi ⚑ ile bildirebilirsin: bildirimde yalnızca senin kurulum numaran ve bildirilen satırın o güne ait kodu kaydedilir. İki farklı oyuncu bildirirse isim o gün herkese gizlenir. Bildirimler de en fazla 90 gün saklanır.

Hata raporları

Yayındaki site ve Android uygulaması, oyunda bir yazılım hatası olursa hatanın teknik mesajını, kodda nerede olduğunu, oyunun sürümünü, uygulama mı web mi olduğunu ve telefonun dil ayarını sunucumuza gönderir; böylece hatayı düzeltebiliriz. Kurulum numarası, oyuncu adı ya da başka kişisel bilgi gönderilmez. Raporlar günlük sayı olarak tutulur ve 30 gün sonra silinir.

Privacy Policy

Blackhole Storm · Lias Labs · Last updated: October 4, 2026

Blackhole Storm has no accounts or analytics. The web version has no ads; the Android app shows ads through Google AdMob (below). We do not sell your data. The game's data stays on your phone; only the world ranking described below (which you can turn off in Settings) and the Google Play purchase acknowledgement send limited information to our server.

Stored on your device

  • Player name and avatar (chosen by you; it does not need to be your real name)
  • Scores, records, level progress, star and diamond balance, achievements, daily missions
  • Settings (music, sound effects, vibration, recording preference)

This data stays in your browser's local storage on your phone and is never sent to a server, except what the world ranking sends (below). Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes it permanently.

World ranking (Daily Storm)

When a Survival run ends and Settings › Join the world ranking is on, the game sends our ranking server (Cloudflare): a random install number made on your device (not linked to you), the player name you typed in the game, your score, the run's length and the date. This is used only to show the daily ranking and reward the top 3; it is kept for at most 90 days and then deleted. No email, location, device ID or advertising ID is sent. Turn the setting off if you don't want to take part; write to us to have your entry removed.

Inappropriate player names are filtered automatically on the server and shown as "PİLOT". You can report a name on the ranking with ⚑: a report stores only your install number and that row's code for the day. If two different players report a name, it is hidden for everyone that day. Reports are also kept for at most 90 days.

Error reports

If a software error happens in the game, the published site and the Android app send its technical message, where in the code it happened, the game version, whether it is the app or the web version, and the phone's language setting to our server so we can fix it. No install number, player name or other personal information is sent. Reports are kept as daily counts and deleted after 30 days.

Sharing you start

  • Results and challenge links: when you tap "Share", your score, player name and the run's seed number are put into a message/link and handed to your phone's share sheet. You decide whether and with whom to send it.
  • Video clips: if enabled in Settings, 60-second runs are recorded on your device. A clip leaves the device only if you save or share it. The microphone is not used; clips contain game audio only.

In-app purchases (Google Play version)

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. Your payment details stay with Google; we never see or store them. To deliver what you bought, the game only keeps the purchase token sent by Google Play on your device. Before anything is delivered, this token is sent with the product name and your install number to our server (Cloudflare), which checks with Google that the purchase is real and paid, makes the acknowledgement Google requires, and keeps the token with the install number so the same purchase cannot be reused on other devices. In the Android app, a one-time Google Play Integrity code may also be sent with ranking scores and prize claims so the server can tell they come from the genuine, unmodified app; it is only checked with Google and not stored. Whether your VIP subscription is active is checked with Google Play at each launch; you can cancel it any time in Google Play › Subscriptions.

Ads (Android app)

The Android app shows rewarded ads (you choose to watch one for a reward) and an occasional short ad between levels; the ad-free product removes them. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (for approximate location), device and app information and how you interact with ads; this is handled as described in Google's How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears at first launch and asks whether you agree to personalised ads; you can change your choice in Settings › Ad privacy options. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings. Ads are limited to content suitable for ages 13 and up.

Permissions

The game uses vibration (can be turned off in Settings). If you allow it, the Android app shows reminder notifications (Laboratory done, fuel full, daily reward; at most 2 a day, never at night). They are planned on your phone, no data is sent for them, and they can be turned off in Settings. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or file access.

Children

The game is designed for ages 13 and up. We never ask anyone, of any age, for an email, phone number, location or real name; the world ranking only receives the nickname you type in the game and a random install number. If you are under 13, don't use your real name as your player name, or turn the world ranking off in Settings.

Data controller and your rights (GDPR / Turkish KVKK)

  • Controller: Lias Labs · liaslabs.games@gmail.com
  • Purpose: showing the world ranking and giving daily prizes; delivering what you buy and the purchase acknowledgement Google requires. The information is not used for anything else.
  • Legal basis: providing the service you asked for and performing the purchase contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), KVKK Art. 5/2-c). Taking part in the world ranking is optional and can be turned off in Settings at any time.
