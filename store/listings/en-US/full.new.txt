Drag a black hole with your finger through a universe built from real Hubble and James Webb images. Swallow anything smaller than you, dodge meteors, and grow from a tiny singularity into a blazing quasar.

HOW IT PLAYS
• One finger: drag, swallow, grow. Easy to start, hard to master
• Every body you swallow makes you bigger, and bigger black holes can swallow bigger worlds
• Chain swallows into combos, fill your Vortex and unleash it at the right moment
• Arcs around your black hole take the hits: lose them all and the level is over

A UNIVERSE TO CROSS
• 5 regions linked by warp gates: Deep Space, Westerlund 2, Orion, Carina and Webb's Cosmic Cliffs
• Planets, moons, comets, pulsars, neutron stars, antimatter, kilonovae, dark matter and more
• A boss every 10 levels: Giant Planet, Red Giant, Supernova and more
• Peak levels and a very rare Golden Arc that lets you swallow everything for a few seconds
• Level map with stars: replay levels and chase three stars on each

POWERS AND THE COSMIC LAB
• Shield, Supernova, Hawking burst, magnet and the quasar jet
• Build your black hole in the Cosmic Lab: new modules open as it evolves
• Expedition, missions, a monthly season and the Mass Tree add long-term goals

DAILY TOURNAMENT
• Survival is the Daily Storm: everyone faces the same storm on equal terms
• Same shields and Supernovas for every player, no diamonds or upgrades in play
• World ranking every day, prizes for the top three and medals to keep
• Daily Race, challenge links and a streak with a weekly chest

LEARN AS YOU PLAY
• Cosmic Atlas: real science about every body you swallow, from NASA and ESA images

ALSO
• Hard Mode for veterans
• 10 languages, no account needed, plays offline
